# Registered-policy learning fixtures

These are independently authored synthetic report/source pairs, not customer
examples or protected holdouts. Target DOCX values were written explicitly;
they were not generated by `foundry.runtime.prepare`. Inspection reads actual
DOCX paragraph/table observations. Each directory supplies `report.docx`,
`transactions.csv`, and an explicit `period.json`.

`ambiguous` reports Q4 2025 against Q4 2024. East moves 10 → 15, North 100 → 200,
South 50 → 60. All three registered policies select North. The observed selected
region therefore cannot identify the rule. Totals are 160 → 275, a change of
115 and displayed growth of 71.9%.

`discriminating` reports Q1 2026 against Q1 2025. East moves 10 → 100, North
990 → 1,000, South 100 → 500. Current revenue selects North; absolute change
selects South; absolute percentage change selects East. The historical target
selects South. Totals are 1,100 → 1,600, a change of 500 and displayed growth
of 45.5%. Both pairs together support only absolute-change selection within
this three-policy envelope.

Each CSV additionally includes one cancelled record and one out-of-period
record with large amounts. These do not contribute to the observed totals.
The tables use explicit English decimal-point EUR and percentage notation.
These fixtures demonstrate ambiguity handling and reconstruction boundaries;
they do not establish arbitrary report learning or unseen-period accuracy.
